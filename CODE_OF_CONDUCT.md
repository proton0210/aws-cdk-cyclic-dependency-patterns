# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We are committed to making participation in this project respectful, safe, and
welcoming for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity,
or location.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, specific, and constructive.
- Critique ideas, code, and evidence rather than people.
- Assume good intent while remaining open to correction.
- Welcome questions and help contributors understand unfamiliar AWS or CDK
  concepts.
- Protect private information, credentials, account identifiers, and customer
  infrastructure details.
- Give credit for ideas, reports, code, and documentation.

## Unacceptable behavior

- Harassment, threats, discrimination, or personal attacks.
- Sexualized language or unwanted attention.
- Trolling, deliberate disruption, or sustained bad-faith argument.
- Publishing another person's private information.
- Sharing credentials, confidential templates, or production data without
  authorization.
- Retaliation against someone who raises a concern in good faith.

## Scope

This code applies to issues, pull requests, discussions, reviews, and other
spaces where someone represents this project.

## Enforcement

Project maintainers may edit or remove content and may warn, temporarily limit,
or permanently exclude participants whose behavior violates this code. Actions
will be proportional to the impact and pattern of behavior.

Report a concern to the maintainers through an appropriate private GitHub
channel when confidentiality is needed. Do not include sensitive personal or
security information in a public issue. Maintainers will review reports in good
faith, limit disclosure to people who need the information, and avoid
retaliation against reporters.
